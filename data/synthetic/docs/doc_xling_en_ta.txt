Clause 1.1: Every registered entity shall submit a quarterly compliance report to the Reserve Bank of India within 30 days of the end of each quarter.

Clause 1.2: The minimum capital requirement for a Category-I Authorised Dealer shall not be less than Rs. 500 crore at all times.

Clause 1.3: No person shall engage in foreign exchange transactions without prior approval of the designated authority under Section 10(1) of the Act.
